Vaultfolio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15th Nov 2026
No accounts No ads No analytics or trackers Data stays on your phone

Summary

Vaultfolio is a portfolio-tracking app. Your holdings, amounts, and notes are encrypted with the PIN you set and stored only on your device's local storage. Vaultfolio does not have a server, does not require sign-up, and does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your personal or financial data. Nothing you enter in the app — holding names, quantities, cost basis, notes — ever leaves your phone.

What stays on your device

  • Every holding, quantity, cost, note, and transaction you add
  • Your 6-digit PIN (used only to encrypt/decrypt your data locally; it is never sent anywhere)
  • Biometric unlock, if you enable it, is handled entirely by your phone's own operating system — Vaultfolio never sees your fingerprint or face data
  • Backups you export are plain files saved to your own device/storage, under your control

What does leave your device, and why

To show you live prices, Vaultfolio looks up a ticker symbol or currency pair (for example, "AAPL" or "USD/AED") against public market-data providers. These lookups contain only the symbol being priced — never your name, your holdings list, your cost basis, or anything that identifies you or your portfolio.

Depending on what you hold, Vaultfolio may query:

  • Yahoo Finance, Google Finance, Twelve Data, TradingView, and financialmodelingprep.com — for stocks, ETFs, and indices
  • Coinbase and CoinGecko — for cryptocurrency prices
  • gold-api.com — for gold and silver prices
  • Frankfurter and open.er-api.com — for currency exchange rates
  • AMFI India (via mfapi.in) — for Indian mutual fund NAVs

These are standard public price-data lookups, the same kind any finance app or website makes. Vaultfolio has no way to attach your identity to these requests, because it never sends anything else.

What Vaultfolio never does

  • No account creation, login, or cloud sync
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking of any kind
  • No advertising, and no sharing of data with advertisers or anyone else
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, or other apps

Children's privacy

Vaultfolio is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect information from children. Since the app collects no personal information from anyone, this applies to all users regardless of age.
